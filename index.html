How to play

  1. Pick a tower or hero from the right panel, then click the grass to place it. You can't build on the track.
  2. Press Start Round to send in the legion. Clear every round to finish the level.
  3. Every trooper that reaches the bastion costs you lives. Kills earn cash for more towers.
  4. You may deploy only 2 heroes per mission — choose them to suit the map.
  5. Click a placed unit to upgrade or sell it.
  6. Clearing rounds earns gems. Spend 150 gems on a crate to unlock the other heroes.
  7. Verdant, Streak and Paragon have clickable ultimates — click them on the map to fire. Each gets 1 use per round, 2 once upgraded, and 3 at max level.
  8. From level 3 the legion sends counter escorts: some suppress heroes that carry a matching tag while they're nearby, others are flatly immune to a damage type. Check the level card before you pick your two heroes — and remember towers are never affected.
  9. Beat all 10 levels across four maps to finish the campaign.
