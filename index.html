How to play

  1. Every mission starts on the squad screen: pick which heroes you're taking before you deploy. Only the ones you choose show up in the shop, so the map screen stays readable.
  2. Pick a unit or hero from the right panel, then click the ground to place it. You can't build on the road.
  3. Press Start Round to send in the legion. Clear every round to finish the mission.
  4. Every trooper that reaches the bastion costs you lives. Kills earn cash — and every round you clear pays a bonus that grows as the mission goes on, so the late rounds are where the big upgrades get paid for.
  5. Hero slots grow with the campaign: one in the first city, two in the second, three from the third on. Units are the backbone; heroes are support.
  6. A hero's ultimate only unlocks once it is fully upgraded, and most get a single use per round. Lighter kits like Streak and Nocturne unlock earlier and get two.
  7. The Relay Mast builds nothing of its own — it widens the range and speeds up the fire of every unit standing inside its ring.
  8. Click a placed unit to upgrade or sell it. Upgrades are flat: 500 / 1500 for a unit, 1000 / 2500 for a hero.
  9. Stars pay gems, and a whole city taken at 3★ pays a bounty. Spend gems on cases to unlock the rest of the roster.
  10. Most heroes have a clickable ultimate — click them on the map, or press Q, to fire.
  11. From mission 3 the legion sends counter escorts: some suppress heroes that carry a matching tag while they're nearby, others are flatly immune to a damage type. The squad screen marks the tags an escort shuts down in red — and units are never affected.
  12. Take all three stars on every mission in a city to open the next one. Six cities, 30 missions.
